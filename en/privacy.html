Privacy Policy

SpesaFast is an Android app for organizing grocery shopping — lists, product categories and supermarket maps — developed and maintained by a single independent developer, not a company.

Last updated: October 5, 2026 Developer: Emiliano Pinto Contact: emi.pinto.pn@gmail.com

1.1 Data stored only on your device

Shopping lists, product categories, supermarket maps and the classification dictionary are stored in a local database on your phone. This data is never sent to a server run by the developer: the developer has no access to its content.

1.2 Google sign-in (optional)

As an optional feature, the app offers signing in with a Google account to save a backup copy of your data and restore it on another device. The Google data access requested is limited to the drive.appdata scope: a private, hidden folder belonging to the app inside the user's Drive, not visible or accessible from other apps, the Drive website, or anyone else — including the developer.

The account's email address is read once, at sign-in time, to display it on the app's Settings screen ("Signed in as ..."). It is not stored on any external server and is not shared with third parties.

1.3 Automatic product classification (optional)

If the user chooses, on their own initiative, to configure credentials for external AI services (Aigway, Google Gemini or Groq), the product names typed into the app may be sent to that service to suggest a category. No other personal data is sent. Use of these services is governed by the respective privacy policies of those third-party providers.

1.4 What we don't do

  • No advertising and no advertising tracking.
  • No usage analytics service.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with third parties, beyond what is described above.

1.5 Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes all local data from the device. The Drive backup can be removed at any time by signing out from the app's Settings screen, or by revoking SpesaFast's access from the Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access page.

1.6 Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from minors.

In short: your data stays on your phone or in your private Drive folder. The developer doesn't see it, sell it, or share it.

1.7 Changes to this policy

Any changes will be published on this same page, with the update date shown at the top.
